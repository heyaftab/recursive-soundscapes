WELCOME TO THE STUDIO

The mathematics of music.

Choose a pattern, adjust its shape, then press Play. The notes are generated from the recursive structure itself — the glowing shapes you see correspond to the notes you hear.

  1. Choose Fibonacci, Sierpinski, Recursive Tree, Koch or Towers of Hanoi.
  2. Shape the depth, tempo, density and scale.
  3. Listen in one of four moods; follow the highlighted nodes.
  4. Save your original composition as a WAV file.

Tip: “Generate new variation” keeps the algorithm but changes the melodic interpretation.
